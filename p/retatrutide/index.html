What Retatrutide is

Phase 3 trials show up to 24% weight loss - highest of any obesity medication. Triple agonist targeting GLP-1, GIP, and glucagon receptors. Creates thermogenic effect alongside appetite suppression.

Mechanism

Retatrutide activates three receptors: GLP-1 (satiety/glucose), GIP (insulin secretion/enhancement), and glucagon (energy expenditure/lipolysis). The glucagon component adds thermogenic fat burning not seen in dual agonists, creating unprecedented weight loss.

Warnings

  • Heart rate: sustained elevation requires monitoring
  • Thyroid cancer: same GLP-1 class precautions
  • Cardiovascular: history requires cardiologist clearance
  • MEN2: contraindicated
  • Pregnancy: discontinue

Administration notes

  • Week 1-4: 2mg weekly (lower start halves GI side effects vs 4mg)
  • Week 5-8: 4mg weekly
  • Week 9-12: 8mg weekly
  • Week 13+: 12mg weekly (highest trial arm, -24.2% at 48 weeks)
  • Titrate every 4 weeks for GI tolerance
  • Monitor resting heart rate - elevation is expected and dose-related
  • Cardiovascular clearance recommended

Common questions

How many units is 8 mg of Retatrutide on an insulin syringe?

53.3 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 30 mg vial is reconstituted with 2 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.533 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 30 mg vial of Retatrutide?

2 ml is the volume used here, giving 15 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 30 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Retatrutide injected?

Weekly. A typical course runs Continuous, which is 48 injections (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is ~1 week.

How many vials does one Retatrutide cycle take?

About 13 30 mg vials for the full course - 48 doses at 8 mg each, 384 mg in total.

Is Retatrutide approved for human use?

Human trial data. Published human clinical trials exist at comparable doses. The compound is not approved for this use, and trial doses were given under supervision.
