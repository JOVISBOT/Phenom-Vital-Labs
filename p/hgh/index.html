What HGH (Human Growth Hormone) is

Synthetic human growth hormone (somatropin) is FDA approved for GH deficiency, muscle wasting, and short bowel syndrome. Decades of clinical use with established protocols. Direct GH administration vs stimulating natural production.

Mechanism

Exogenous HGH directly elevates circulating growth hormone and IGF-1 levels without relying on pituitary function. It binds to GH receptors throughout the body, promoting anabolism, lipolysis, and anti-aging effects. Bypasses natural regulatory mechanisms.

Common questions

How many units is 2 IU of HGH (Human Growth Hormone) on an insulin syringe?

40 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 10 IU vial is reconstituted with 2 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.4 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 10 IU vial of HGH (Human Growth Hormone)?

2 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 IU per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 10 IU however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is HGH (Human Growth Hormone) injected?

Daily. A typical course runs 6 months minimum, which is 168 injections (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is 2-4 hours (IV).

How many vials does one HGH (Human Growth Hormone) cycle take?

About 34 10 IU vials for the full course - 168 doses at 2 IU each, 336 IU in total.
