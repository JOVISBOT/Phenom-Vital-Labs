What HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin) is

HMG contains both FSH and LH, making it superior to HCG alone for fertility and testosterone production. Used in fertility treatments and post-cycle therapy (PCT) to restore natural testosterone production.

Mechanism

HMG contains follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH) and luteinizing hormone (LH). FSH stimulates Sertoli cells and sperm production, while LH stimulates Leydig cells to produce testosterone. More comprehensive than HCG which only mimics LH.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Contains both FSH and LH
  • Superior to HCG for fertility
  • Restores natural testosterone
  • Supports sperm production
  • Used in fertility clinics worldwide

Reported drawbacks

  • Requires injection
  • More expensive than HCG
  • Less convenient than HCG
  • May cause testicular enlargement (desired but uncomfortable)

Warnings

  • Pregnancy: contraindicated (fertility drug)
  • Hormone sensitive cancers: avoid
  • Ovarian cysts: risk in women
  • Testosterone: may aromatize to estrogen
  • General: hormone therapy - monitoring required

Administration notes

  • Subcutaneous or IM injection
  • 3 times per week (every other day)
  • Supplied as 75 IU vials - higher doses are several vials pooled into one syringe
  • Use during PCT or fertility treatment
  • Monitor testosterone and estrogen
  • Cycle 3-6 weeks maximum

Common questions

How many units is 150 IU of HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin) on an insulin syringe?

100 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 75 IU vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 1 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 75 IU vial of HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin)?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 75 IU per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 75 IU however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin) injected?

3x weekly. A typical course runs 3-6 weeks, which is 9-18 injections depending where in that range you dose. Reported half-life is Unknown.

How many vials does one HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin) cycle take?

18-36 75 IU vials for the full course - 9-18 doses at 150 IU each.
