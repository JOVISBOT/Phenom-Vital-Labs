What AOD-9604 is

AOD-9604 is the C-terminus fragment of HGH (amino acids 177-191). Clinical trials show fat oxidation without affecting blood glucose or IGF-1. Originally developed as anti-obesity drug with favorable safety profile.

Mechanism

AOD-9604 mimics the fat-burning region of natural GH without the growth-promoting or blood sugar effects. It stimulates lipolysis (fat breakdown) and inhibits lipogenesis (fat storage) through modulation of beta-3-adrenergic receptors and other pathways.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Burns fat without affecting blood glucose
  • Does NOT raise IGF-1 (safer than GH)
  • No impact on insulin sensitivity
  • No water retention
  • No impact on natural GH production
  • FDA granted Generally Recognized As Safe (GRAS) status

Reported drawbacks

  • Requires multiple daily injections
  • Effects more modest than GLP-1 drugs
  • Best on empty stomach (timing constraint)
  • Must be combined with diet for results
  • Shorter half-life requires frequent dosing
  • Less potent than modern weight loss peptides

Warnings

  • Pregnancy: insufficient data
  • Cancer: theoretically safer (no IGF-1 elevation)
  • Diabetes: does NOT affect glucose (safe)
  • Heart conditions: consult physician
  • Generally recognized as safest weight loss peptide

Administration notes

  • Morning on empty stomach (pre-breakfast)
  • Pre-workout for enhanced fat oxidation
  • Before bed (optional third dose)
  • Subcutaneous abdominal fat preferred
  • Works best with caloric deficit and exercise

Common questions

How many units is 0.5 mg of AOD-9604 on an insulin syringe?

30 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 3 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.3 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of AOD-9604?

3 ml is the volume used here, giving 1.67 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is AOD-9604 injected?

2-3x daily. A typical course runs Continuous OK, which is 168-252 injections depending where in that range you dose (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is Unknown (short).

How many vials does one AOD-9604 cycle take?

17-26 5 mg vials for the full course - 168-252 doses at 0.5 mg each.

Is AOD-9604 approved for human use?

Human trial data. Published human clinical trials exist at comparable doses. The compound is not approved for this use, and trial doses were given under supervision.
