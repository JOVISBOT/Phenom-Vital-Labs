What Dermorphin is

Dermorphin is a heptapeptide found in South American tree frog skin with potent mu-opioid activity (40x morphine). Research only - not approved for human use. Extremely potent opioid effects with short duration.

Mechanism

Dermorphin is a potent and selective mu-opioid receptor (MOR) agonist with approximately 40x the potency of morphine. It produces analgesia through the same pathways as traditional opioids but with different selectivity and side effect profiles.

Common questions

How many units is 2,000 mcg of Dermorphin on an insulin syringe?

40 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.4 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of Dermorphin?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.
