What Dulaglutide (Trulicity) is

FDA approved 2014 for type 2 diabetes. Weekly GLP-1 agonist with proven cardiovascular benefits (REDUCES major CV events). REWIND trial showed reduced stroke/MI in diabetics with CV risk. Now studied for weight loss and prediabetes.

Mechanism

Dulaglutide is a long-acting GLP-1 receptor agonist with albumin-binding domain for extended half-life. It enhances glucose-dependent insulin secretion, suppresses glucagon, slows gastric emptying, and promotes satiety. Cardiovascular benefits beyond glucose lowering.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • FDA approved with CV benefits
  • Proven to reduce stroke/MI/death
  • Weekly injection (convenient)
  • Weight loss benefits
  • Prediabetes treatment
  • Lower cost than newer GLP-1s

Reported drawbacks

  • Less weight loss than semaglutide (~10% vs 15%)
  • GI side effects common
  • Injectable only
  • Thyroid box warning
  • Requires injection training

Warnings

  • Thyroid C-cell tumors: boxed warning
  • Pancreatitis: monitor symptoms
  • Gastroparesis: may worsen
  • Renal: caution with impairment
  • Pregnancy: discontinue
  • Gallbladder: stone risk

Administration notes

  • Weekly subcutaneous injection from a single-dose pre-filled pen
  • No reconstitution - the pen is supplied ready to inject
  • Dose is fixed by the pen strength: 0.75, 1.5, 3 or 4.5 mg
  • Any time of day, same day each week
  • Titrate 0.75mg -> 1.5mg -> 3mg -> 4.5mg at 4-week intervals as tolerated
  • Take with food if nausea occurs
  • Monitor blood glucose and weight
