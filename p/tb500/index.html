What TB 500 is

Thymosin Beta-4 has been extensively studied in clinical trials for wound healing and cardiac repair. FDA has granted orphan drug status for TB-4 in epidermolysis bullosa. Research shows it accelerates keratinocyte migration and promotes actin polymerization in healing tissues.

Mechanism

TB-500 binds to actin monomers, promoting cell migration and tissue regeneration. It regulates G-actin/F-actin ratios, enabling cellular mobility critical for wound closure. Additionally, it upregulates vascular endothelial growth factor and promotes stem cell differentiation.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Systemic healing effects throughout body
  • Only 2 injections per week required
  • Promotes stem cell migration and differentiation
  • Improves flexibility and reduces tissue fibrosis
  • Shown cardioprotective effects in animal studies
  • Effective for chronic injuries unresponsive to other treatments

Reported drawbacks

  • Requires loading phase for optimal results
  • More expensive than BPC-157
  • Headache reported in some users
  • Redness at injection site common
  • Slower onset compared to BPC-157

Warnings

  • Active malignancy: promotes cell proliferation
  • Heart conditions: monitor if cardiac issues present
  • Cancer history: discuss with oncologist
  • Pregnancy: insufficient safety data

Administration notes

  • Loading: 2-2.5mg twice weekly (4-5mg/wk) for the first 4-6 weeks
  • Maintenance: 2-2.5mg once weekly thereafter
  • Any fatty area acceptable (abdomen, thigh, glute)
  • Best stacked with BPC-157 for acute injuries

Common questions

How many units is 2,000 mcg of TB 500 on an insulin syringe?

40 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.4 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of TB 500?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is TB 500 injected?

2x weekly. A typical course runs 6 on, 4 off, which is 12 injections. Reported half-life is Long (days).

How many vials does one TB 500 cycle take?

About 5 5 mg vials for the full course - 12 doses at 2,000 mcg each, 24 mg in total.
