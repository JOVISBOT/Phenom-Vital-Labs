What CJC1295 NO DAC + Ipamorelin Blend is

Pre-mixed blend: CJC-1295 NO DAC 5mg + Ipamorelin 5mg per vial. Combines pulsatile GH release of Mod GRF with selective GHRP action of Ipamorelin.

Mechanism

Dual mechanism: CJC NO DAC creates pulsatile GH elevation via GHRH stimulation. Ipamorelin adds selective ghrelin receptor activation without cortisol/prolactin elevation. Together they optimize GH secretion patterns.

Administration notes

  • Contains 5mg CJC-1295 NO DAC + 5mg Ipamorelin (10mg total)
  • Convention is 100-300mcg of EACH peptide per injection; 200mcg is typical
  • CJC-1295 NO DAC saturates the GHRH receptor near 1mcg/kg - past that, side effects scale but GH release does not
  • Inject once daily before bed on empty stomach
  • Evening dosing rides the natural overnight GH pulse
  • Empty stomach: wait 30 min after injection before eating

Common questions

How many units is 0.4 mg of CJC1295 NO DAC + Ipamorelin Blend on an insulin syringe?

12 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 10 mg vial is reconstituted with 3 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.12 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 10 mg vial of CJC1295 NO DAC + Ipamorelin Blend?

3 ml is the volume used here, giving 3.33 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 10 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is CJC1295 NO DAC + Ipamorelin Blend injected?

How many vials does one CJC1295 NO DAC + Ipamorelin Blend cycle take?
