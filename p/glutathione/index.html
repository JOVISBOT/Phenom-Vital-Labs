More water means a bigger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose. A dose that lands near the 2-unit mark is one half-mark misread away from a 25% dosing error. Past 100 units the dose no longer fits a U-100 barrel at all — that is two injections, not a bigger syringe. A concentration flagged may not dissolve is the opposite failure: the draw is small and readable, but the powder is being asked to go into solution above roughly 400 mg/ml, where lyophilised material generally stops doing so. Add more water, or confirm the figure against the vendor's own reconstitution note.

What Glutathione (Reduced) is

Mechanism

Glutathione is a tripeptide (glutamate-cysteine-glycine) that neutralizes free radicals, supports phase II liver detoxification, and regenerates other antioxidants (vitamin C, E). It binds to toxins for elimination and maintains cellular redox balance.

Reported effects and risks

  • Skin brightening/anti-melanin effects
  • Heavy metal chelation support

Common questions

How many units is 200 mg of Glutathione (Reduced) on an insulin syringe?

33.3 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 600 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.333 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 600 mg vial of Glutathione (Reduced)?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 600 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 600 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Glutathione (Reduced) injected?

2-3x weekly. A typical course runs Continuous OK, which is 24-36 injections depending where in that range you dose (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is Unknown (minutes).

How many vials does one Glutathione (Reduced) cycle take?

8-12 600 mg vials for the full course - 24-36 doses at 200 mg each.
