What FOXO4-DRI (Senolytic) is

FOXO4-DRI is a senolytic peptide that induces apoptosis in senescent ("zombie") cells. Research published in Cell (2017) showed extended lifespan and restored physical capacity in aging mice. Selectively targets harmful senescent cells while sparing healthy cells.

Mechanism

FOXO4-DRI interferes with the FOXO4-p53 interaction that keeps senescent cells alive. By blocking this interaction, p53 is freed to trigger apoptosis (programmed cell death) specifically in senescent cells. This clears aged cells without affecting healthy tissue.

How many units is 2 mg of FOXO4-DRI (Senolytic) on an insulin syringe?

40 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.4 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of FOXO4-DRI (Senolytic)?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.
