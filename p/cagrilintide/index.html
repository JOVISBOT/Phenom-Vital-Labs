What Cagrilintide is

Cagrilintide is a long-acting amylin analog developed by Novo Nordisk. Phase 3 trials show significant weight loss when combined with semaglutide (CagriSema). Amylin acts on brain satiety centers and slows gastric emptying.

Mechanism

Cagrilintide mimics human amylin, a hormone co-secreted with insulin from pancreatic beta cells. It acts on amylin receptors in the brainstem to induce satiety, reduces glucagon secretion, and slows gastric emptying. The long-acting formulation allows weekly dosing.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Long-acting weekly injection
  • Potent appetite suppression
  • Combines well with semaglutide (CagriSema)
  • Slows gastric emptying
  • May improve glycemic control
  • Novel mechanism (amylin vs GLP-1)

Reported drawbacks

  • Nausea common initially
  • Must titrate slowly
  • Limited as monotherapy (best combined)
  • Injectable only
  • Newer with less long-term data

Warnings

  • Pancreatitis: monitor for symptoms
  • Gastroparesis: may worsen
  • Thyroid: monitor (class effect)
  • Pregnancy: contraindicated
  • Gallbladder: risk of stones

Administration notes

  • Weekly subcutaneous injection, same day each week
  • Phase 2 titration: 0.3mg -> 0.6mg -> 1.2mg -> 2.4mg -> 4.5mg
  • Escalate every 2 weeks as tolerated - do not start at the top tier
  • 4.5mg weekly was the most effective arm (-10.8% body weight at 26 weeks)
  • Often combined with semaglutide (see Cagrisema) rather than run alone
  • Monitor for nausea - may need antiemetics during escalation

Common questions

How many units is 2.4 mg of Cagrilintide on an insulin syringe?

48 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 10 mg vial is reconstituted with 2 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.48 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 10 mg vial of Cagrilintide?

2 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 10 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Cagrilintide injected?

Weekly. A typical course runs Continuous, which is 26 injections. Reported half-life is ~7 days.

How many vials does one Cagrilintide cycle take?

About 7 10 mg vials for the full course - 26 doses at 2.4 mg each, 62.4 mg in total.

Is Cagrilintide approved for human use?

Human trial data. Published human clinical trials exist at comparable doses. The compound is not approved for this use, and trial doses were given under supervision.
