What GHRP-2 is

Considered the most potent of the GHRP family with high affinity for the ghrelin receptor. Studies show 3-5x GH release compared to baseline. However, it raises cortisol and prolactin, requiring careful consideration for users sensitive to these hormones.

Mechanism

GHRP-2 is a potent synthetic agonist of the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a). It triggers massive pulsatile GH release by mimicking ghrelin, the "hunger hormone." Unlike Ipamorelin, it also stimulates ACTH and prolactin release, creating a more pronounced but less selective GH response.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Most potent GHRP (stronger than GHRP-6)
  • Dramatic GH elevation (3-5x baseline)
  • Significant appetite stimulation (for bulking)
  • Enhances muscle fullness and pump
  • Rapid onset of effects
  • Cost-effective GH secretagogue

Reported drawbacks

  • Raises cortisol (stress hormone)
  • Elevates prolactin (can cause gynecomastia)
  • Strong hunger stimulation may be unwanted
  • May cause lethargy after injection
  • Water retention common
  • Desensitization with prolonged use

Warnings

  • High cortisol: caution with adrenal issues
  • Elevated prolactin: monitor for gynecomastia
  • Diabetes: significant glucose impact
  • Anxiety disorders: may worsen symptoms
  • Cancer history: standard GH precautions

Administration notes

  • Administer 2-3 times daily on empty stomach
  • Pre-workout injection enhances training pump
  • Prepare for significant hunger after dosing
  • Cycle 8-12 weeks maximum before break
  • Consider P5P supplement to manage prolactin

Common questions

How many units is 200 mcg of GHRP-2 on an insulin syringe?

12 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 3 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.12 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of GHRP-2?

3 ml is the volume used here, giving 1.67 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is GHRP-2 injected?

2-3x daily. A typical course runs 8-12 on, 4 off, which is 112-252 injections depending where in that range you dose. Reported half-life is 15-30 minutes.

How many vials does one GHRP-2 cycle take?

5-11 5 mg vials for the full course - 112-252 doses at 200 mcg each.

Is GHRP-2 approved for human use?

Human trial data. Published human clinical trials exist at comparable doses. The compound is not approved for this use, and trial doses were given under supervision.
