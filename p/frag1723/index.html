What Fragment 17-23 is

Fragment 17-23 is a modified version of the HGH Fragment 176-191 with additional amino acids. Research suggests enhanced fat oxidation and metabolic effects compared to the shorter fragment. Limited clinical data available.

Mechanism

Fragment 17-23 contains the full fat-burning region of HGH (amino acids 177-191) plus additional stabilizing residues. It mimics the lipolytic effects of natural GH without affecting blood glucose or IGF-1 levels, potentially with enhanced stability.

Common questions

How many units is 400 mcg of Fragment 17-23 on an insulin syringe?

24 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 3 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.24 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of Fragment 17-23?

3 ml is the volume used here, giving 1.67 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Fragment 17-23 injected?

2-3x daily. A typical course runs Continuous OK, which is 168-252 injections depending where in that range you dose (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is Unknown.

How many vials does one Fragment 17-23 cycle take?

14-21 5 mg vials for the full course - 168-252 doses at 400 mcg each.
