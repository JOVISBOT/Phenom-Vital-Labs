What CJC-1295 NO DAC (Mod GRF 1-29) is

CJC-1295 without DAC (also called Modified GRF 1-29) is the shorter-acting version. Clinical research shows it creates a natural GH pulse without the prolonged elevation of the DAC version. Preferred by some for more natural GH patterns and less water retention.

Mechanism

Mod GRF 1-29 is a modified GHRH (1-29) with enhanced stability. It stimulates pituitary GH release in a pulsatile fashion similar to natural GHRH. The lack of DAC means shorter duration but more natural GH patterns without prolonged elevation.

Common questions

How many units is 400 mcg of CJC-1295 NO DAC (Mod GRF 1-29) on an insulin syringe?

40 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 2 mg vial is reconstituted with 2 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.4 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 2 mg vial of CJC-1295 NO DAC (Mod GRF 1-29)?

2 ml is the volume used here, giving 1 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 2 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is CJC-1295 NO DAC (Mod GRF 1-29) injected?

1-3x daily. A typical course runs 12 on, 4 off, which is 252 injections. Reported half-life is 30 minutes.

How many vials does one CJC-1295 NO DAC (Mod GRF 1-29) cycle take?

About 51 2 mg vials for the full course - 252 doses at 400 mcg each, 100.8 mg in total.
