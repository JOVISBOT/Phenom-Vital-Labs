What Crystagen is

Crystagen is a synthetic tripeptide bioregulator for the immune system. Russian clinical studies show improved immune response, enhanced T-cell function, and normalization of immune activity in patients with immune dysregulation.

Mechanism

Crystagen acts on immune cells to normalize proliferation and differentiation. It supports thymus function and T-cell maturation, helping regulate both underactive and overactive immune responses for balanced immune surveillance.

Common questions

How many units is 5,000 mcg of Crystagen on an insulin syringe?

50 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 10 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.5 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 10 mg vial of Crystagen?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 10 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 10 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Crystagen injected?

Daily. A typical course runs 20 days every 6 months, which is 20 injections. Reported half-life is Unknown.

How many vials does one Crystagen cycle take?

About 10 10 mg vials for the full course - 20 doses at 5,000 mcg each, 100 mg in total.
