What PEG MGF is

Mechano Growth Factor (MGF) is the splice variant of IGF-1 produced in response to mechanical stress (training). PEGylation extends its half-life from minutes to days. Research shows enhanced satellite cell activation and muscle repair after damage.

Mechanism

PEG-MGF activates satellite cells and promotes muscle stem cell proliferation. The PEG (polyethylene glycol) modification prevents degradation and extends circulation time. Works primarily through local autocrine/paracrine mechanisms at muscle tissue level.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Activates muscle satellite cells for repair/growth
  • Extended half-life vs regular MGF (PEGylation)
  • Works locally in trained muscles
  • Enhances recovery between training sessions
  • Promotes muscle cell nuclei addition
  • Lower systemic effects than IGF-1 LR3

Reported drawbacks

  • Intramuscular injection into trained muscle required
  • Timing critical: post-workout within hours
  • Less potent than IGF-1 LR3
  • Research still evolving
  • Site-specific effects require strategic injection
  • Limited long-term safety data

Warnings

  • Cancer: may promote cell proliferation
  • Diabetes: monitor glucose (IGF-1 related)
  • Heart tissue: theoretical cardiac concerns
  • Injection technique: must be intramuscular
  • Pregnancy: insufficient data

Administration notes

  • Inject INTO the muscle trained that day
  • Timing: within 1-2 hours post-workout optimal
  • Intramuscular injection (not subcutaneous)
  • Typically 2-3 times per week per muscle group
  • Can combine with systemic IGF-1 or GH peptides

Common questions

How many units is 200 mcg of PEG MGF on an insulin syringe?

30 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 2 mg vial is reconstituted with 3 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.3 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 2 mg vial of PEG MGF?

3 ml is the volume used here, giving 0.67 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 2 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is PEG MGF injected?

2-3x weekly. A typical course runs 4 on, 2 off, which is 12 injections. Reported half-life is Several days.

How many vials does one PEG MGF cycle take?

About 2 2 mg vials for the full course - 12 doses at 200 mcg each, 2.4 mg in total.
