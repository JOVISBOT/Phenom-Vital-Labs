What Gonadorelin is

Gonadorelin is synthetic GnRH that stimulates the pituitary to release LH and FSH. Used for fertility treatments, hypogonadism testing, and PCT. Short-acting version requires pulsatile administration unlike long-acting analogs.

Mechanism

Gonadorelin binds to GnRH receptors in the anterior pituitary, stimulating pulsatile release of LH and FSH. This stimulates gonadal function and testosterone production. Short half-life requires frequent administration to maintain natural pulsatility.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Stimulates natural LH/FSH
  • Diagnostic and therapeutic
  • Preserves fertility
  • Natural hormone restoration
  • Can restart HPTA

Reported drawbacks

  • Very short half-life
  • Multiple injections daily
  • Desensitization risk if continuous
  • Requires precise timing
  • Less convenient than analogs

Warnings

  • Hormone-sensitive tumors: contraindicated
  • Pregnancy: contraindicated
  • Continuous use: causes suppression
  • General: pulsatile required

Administration notes

  • Subcutaneous injection
  • Every 2 hours for pulse therapy
  • Or continuous pump for fertility
  • Short courses (2 weeks max)
  • Monitor testosterone and LH/FSH

Common questions

How many units is 200 mcg of Gonadorelin on an insulin syringe?

30 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 2 mg vial is reconstituted with 3 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.3 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 2 mg vial of Gonadorelin?

3 ml is the volume used here, giving 0.67 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 2 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Gonadorelin injected?

Multiple daily. A typical course runs As directed, which is 42 injections (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is ~4 minutes.

How many vials does one Gonadorelin cycle take?

About 5 2 mg vials for the full course - 42 doses at 200 mcg each, 8.4 mg in total.

Is Gonadorelin approved for human use?

Human trial data. Published human clinical trials exist at comparable doses. The compound is not approved for this use, and trial doses were given under supervision.
