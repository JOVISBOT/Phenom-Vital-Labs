What Tesamorelin is

FDA approved (2010) specifically for HIV-associated lipodystrophy. Clinical trials showed significant reduction in visceral adipose tissue (VAT) by 18% over 26 weeks. Also improves triglycerides and cholesterol markers. Only peptide specifically indicated for abdominal fat reduction.

Mechanism

Tesamorelin is a synthetic GHRH analog that stimulates endogenous GH production. It specifically targets visceral fat through enhanced lipolysis and improved metabolic function. The peptide also improves lipid profiles and may enhance insulin sensitivity.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • FDA approved for visceral fat reduction
  • Targets stubborn belly fat specifically
  • Improves triglycerides and HDL cholesterol
  • 18% reduction in visceral adipose tissue in trials
  • Also increases IGF-1 for anabolic benefits
  • Better tolerated than synthetic GH

Reported drawbacks

  • Daily injection required
  • Injection site reactions common (redness
  • itching)
  • Most expensive GH secretagogue
  • Joint pain reported in some users
  • May cause fluid retention initially

Warnings

  • Active malignancy: strictly contraindicated
  • Pregnancy/breastfeeding: contraindicated
  • Hypothyroidism: requires monitoring
  • Diabetes: monitor glucose levels
  • HIV patients: specifically studied population

Administration notes

  • Once-daily subcutaneous injection into the abdomen
  • These three are the labelled daily doses of the three formulations, not an escalating ladder
  • EGRIFTA WR 1.28mg, EGRIFTA SV 1.4mg and the original EGRIFTA 2mg give similar systemic exposure
  • Rotate injection sites to limit local reactions
  • Effect on visceral fat is assessed at 6 months, not weeks
  • Reconstitute with the vial size and volume set above

Common questions

How many units is 1.4 mg of Tesamorelin on an insulin syringe?

28 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.28 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of Tesamorelin?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Tesamorelin injected?

Daily. A typical course runs 12 on, 4 off, which is 84 injections. Reported half-life is 30-60 minutes.

How many vials does one Tesamorelin cycle take?

About 24 5 mg vials for the full course - 84 doses at 1.4 mg each, 117.6 mg in total.

Is Tesamorelin approved for human use?

FDA-approved, label dose. An approved product containing this active ingredient is marketed in the US. No dose offered here exceeds a strength or dose on that label.
