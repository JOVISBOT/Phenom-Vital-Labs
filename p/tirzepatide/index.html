What Tirzepatide is

SURPASS trials show 20%+ weight loss, superior to Semaglutide. Dual mechanism targeting both GIP and GLP-1 receptors. FDA approved 2022 for diabetes, 2023 for obesity. Faster and greater weight loss than single-hormone approaches.

Mechanism

Tirzepatide is the first dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist. GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide) enhances GLP-1 effects. Combined action provides superior glucose control, appetite suppression, and fat oxidation compared to GLP-1 alone.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • More potent than Semaglutide (20%+ weight loss)
  • Dual mechanism (GIP + GLP-1)
  • Faster onset of weight reduction
  • Better glycemic control than GLP-1 alone
  • Once weekly injection
  • Improved lipid profiles

Reported drawbacks

  • More GI side effects than Semaglutide
  • Nausea
  • vomiting
  • diarrhea common
  • Higher cost than Semaglutide
  • Requires gradual dose escalation
  • Injection site reactions reported
  • Newer: less long-term safety data

Warnings

  • Thyroid cancer: same precautions as other GLP-1s
  • MEN2: absolute contraindication
  • Pancreatitis: history requires monitoring
  • Gallbladder: increased risk
  • Gastroparesis: may worsen
  • Pregnancy: discontinue

Administration notes

  • Week 1-4: 2.5mg weekly
  • Week 5-8: 5mg weekly
  • Week 9-12: 7.5mg weekly
  • Week 13+: 10-15mg weekly (max)
  • Subcutaneous injection weekly
  • Monitor for GI tolerance at each escalation

Common questions

How many units is 5 mg of Tirzepatide on an insulin syringe?

25 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 20 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.25 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 20 mg vial of Tirzepatide?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 20 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 20 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Tirzepatide injected?

Weekly. A typical course runs Continuous, which is 20 injections (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is 5 days (weekly dosing).

How many vials does one Tirzepatide cycle take?

About 5 20 mg vials for the full course - 20 doses at 5 mg each, 100 mg in total.

Is Tirzepatide approved for human use?

FDA-approved, label dose. An approved product containing this active ingredient is marketed in the US. No dose offered here exceeds a strength or dose on that label.
