What Follistatin 344 is

Follistatin inhibits myostatin, the protein that limits muscle growth. Research in muscular dystrophy and muscle wasting shows dramatic increases in muscle mass when myostatin is blocked. Some subjects gained 20%+ lean mass in studies.

Mechanism

Follistatin binds to and neutralizes myostatin (GDF-8) and activin A, removing the "brakes" on muscle growth. By inhibiting these negative regulators, it allows muscles to grow beyond normal genetic limits. Also affects follicle-stimulating hormone.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Blocks myostatin (muscle growth limiter)
  • Potential for muscle mass beyond genetic limits
  • Research shows dramatic gains in muscle wasting
  • May improve muscle quality in aging
  • Only 1-2 injections per week needed
  • Long-lasting effects per injection

Reported drawbacks

  • Very expensive
  • Limited human clinical data
  • May affect reproductive hormones (FSH)
  • Ethical concerns in competitive sports
  • Unknown long-term safety profile
  • Requires careful sourcing (often faked)

Warnings

  • Reproductive: affects FSH and fertility
  • Heart: theoretical cardiac muscle concerns
  • Tendons: rapid muscle growth may outpace tendon strength
  • Cancer: removing growth limits theoretically risky
  • Pregnancy: absolute contraindication

Administration notes

  • Subcutaneous injection 1-2 times per week
  • Effects are systemic (not site-specific)
  • Cycle length typically 10-12 weeks
  • Often stacked with IGF-1 for synergistic growth
  • Monitor for tendon issues as muscles grow faster

Common questions

How many units is 100 mcg of Follistatin 344 on an insulin syringe?

30 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 1 mg vial is reconstituted with 3 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.3 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 1 mg vial of Follistatin 344?

3 ml is the volume used here, giving 0.33 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 1 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Follistatin 344 injected?

1-2x weekly. A typical course runs 10 on, 6 off, which is 10-20 injections depending where in that range you dose. Reported half-life is Unknown (hours).

How many vials does one Follistatin 344 cycle take?

1-2 1 mg vials for the full course - 10-20 doses at 100 mcg each.
