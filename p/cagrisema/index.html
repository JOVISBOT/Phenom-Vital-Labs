What Cagrisema (Cagrilintide + Semaglutide) is

Pre-mixed blend: Cagrilintide 2.5mg + Semaglutide 2.5mg per vial. Combines amylin analog (Cagrilintide) appetite suppression with GLP-1 (Semaglutide) effects. CagriSema Phase 3 trials show superior weight loss to either drug alone.

Mechanism

Dual mechanism: Cagrilintide mimics amylin for satiety and slowed gastric emptying. Semaglutide activates GLP-1 receptors for appetite suppression and enhanced insulin secretion. Together they provide complementary pathways for weight management.

  • Starting dose: 0.5mg total = 0.25mg of EACH per week

  • Titrate every 4 weeks - both components escalate together
  • Monitor for nausea - may need antiemetics initially

Common questions

How many units is 2.4 mg of Cagrisema (Cagrilintide + Semaglutide) on an insulin syringe?

48 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.48 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of Cagrisema (Cagrilintide + Semaglutide)?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is Cagrisema (Cagrilintide + Semaglutide) injected?

Weekly. A typical course runs Continuous, which is 16 injections (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is ~7 days (Cagrilintide) + 7 days (Semaglutide).

How many vials does one Cagrisema (Cagrilintide + Semaglutide) cycle take?

About 8 5 mg vials for the full course - 16 doses at 2.4 mg each, 38.4 mg in total.
