What AICAR (Acadesine) is

AICAR is an AMPK (AMP-activated protein kinase) activator studied for metabolic benefits, exercise mimetics, and cardioprotection. Research shows enhanced fat oxidation, improved insulin sensitivity, and endurance enhancement in animal studies.

Mechanism

AICAR is taken up by cells and converted to ZMP, which mimics AMP and activates AMPK - the cellular energy sensor. AMPK activation promotes fat oxidation, glucose uptake, mitochondrial biogenesis, and autophagy. Called "exercise in a pill" in research.

Common questions

How many units is 25 mg of AICAR (Acadesine) on an insulin syringe?

50 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 50 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.5 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 50 mg vial of AICAR (Acadesine)?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 50 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 50 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is AICAR (Acadesine) injected?

Daily. A typical course runs 2 weeks max, 4-8 off, which is 14 injections. Reported half-life is Unknown.

How many vials does one AICAR (Acadesine) cycle take?

About 7 50 mg vials for the full course - 14 doses at 25 mg each, 350 mg in total.
