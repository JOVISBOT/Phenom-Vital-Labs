What BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend is

Pre-mixed blend: BPC-157 5mg + TB-500 5mg per vial. Combines local tissue repair with systemic stem cell activation.

Mechanism

BPC-157 promotes local angiogenesis and collagen synthesis at injury sites, while TB-500 mobilizes stem cells and enhances systemic tissue regeneration through actin regulation.

Common questions

How many units is 1 mg of BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend on an insulin syringe?

20 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 10 mg vial is reconstituted with 2 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.2 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 10 mg vial of BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend?

2 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 10 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend injected?

5x weekly. A typical course runs 6 on, 4 off, which is 30 injections. Reported half-life is Mixed (4hrs + days).

How many vials does one BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend cycle take?

About 3 10 mg vials for the full course - 30 doses at 1 mg each, 30 mg in total.
