What PT-141 (Bremelanotide) is

FDA approved 2019 for premenopausal women with hypoactive sexual desire disorder (HSDD). Works on melanocortin receptors in CNS unlike Viagra (vascular). Clinical trials show significant improvement in sexual desire and satisfaction.

Mechanism

PT-141 is a synthetic melanocortin analog that activates MC3R and MC4R receptors in the central nervous system. Unlike PDE5 inhibitors (Viagra), it works on desire pathways in the brain rather than blood flow, addressing psychological/libido aspects of sexual dysfunction.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • FDA approved for HSDD in women (2019)
  • Works via CNS (brain) not vascular system
  • Effective when Viagra/Cialis fail
  • Enhances sexual desire
  • not just function
  • Works in both men and women
  • On-demand dosing (not daily)

Reported drawbacks

  • Nausea very common (40% in trials)
  • Facial flushing
  • Injection required (no oral form)
  • Expensive
  • FDA approval limited to women (off-label for men)
  • Must plan timing (30-60 min onset)

Warnings

  • Blood pressure: transient elevation common
  • Cardiovascular disease: consult physician
  • Nausea: severe in some patients
  • Pregnancy: insufficient data
  • Kidney/liver disease: use caution
  • Alcohol: interaction studies incomplete

Administration notes

  • Inject 45-60 minutes before sexual activity
  • Subcutaneous abdominal or thigh injection
  • Start with 0.5mg to assess nausea
  • Can increase to 1mg, then to 1.75mg - the approved Vyleesi dose and the ceiling here
  • No more than one dose in 24 hours, and no more than 8 doses a month
  • Take with anti-nausea medication if needed
  • Effects last 2-4 hours typically

Common questions

How many units is 1 mg of PT-141 (Bremelanotide) on an insulin syringe?

20 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 10 mg vial is reconstituted with 2 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.2 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 10 mg vial of PT-141 (Bremelanotide)?

2 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 10 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is PT-141 (Bremelanotide) injected?

As needed (before sexual activity). A typical course runs As needed, which is 1 injection (assumed - the record states no fixed course). Reported half-life is 2-3 hours.

How many vials does one PT-141 (Bremelanotide) cycle take?

About 1 10 mg vial for the full course - 1 dose at 1 mg each, 1 mg in total.
