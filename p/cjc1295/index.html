What CJC1295 DAC is

Clinical studies demonstrate sustained GH elevation for up to 6 days after single injection. The DAC (Drug Affinity Complex) extends half-life from minutes to days by binding to albumin. Research shows 2-10x increase in GH levels with minimal side effects.

Mechanism

CJC-1295 is a GHRH (growth hormone releasing hormone) analog with DAC modification that significantly extends its half-life. It stimulates the pituitary to release GH in a more sustained manner compared to natural GHRH. The DAC component binds to albumin, creating a depot effect.

Reported effects and risks

Reported benefits

  • Only 2 injections per week needed
  • Elevates baseline GH for sustained effect
  • Long half-life due to DAC modification
  • Synergistic with GHRPs like Ipamorelin
  • Improves body composition over time
  • Enhanced recovery and sleep quality

Reported drawbacks

  • Water retention common initially
  • Less selective than Ipamorelin
  • Can cause numbness/tingling in extremities
  • Requires cycling to maintain effectiveness
  • More expensive than regular CJC-1295 (no DAC)

Warnings

  • Cancer history: elevates IGF-1
  • Water retention: caution with heart/kidney issues
  • Diabetes: monitor blood glucose
  • Carpal tunnel syndrome: may worsen symptoms

Administration notes

  • Subcutaneous injection 2x per week (e.g.
  • Monday/Thursday)
  • Can cause initial water retention (usually temporary)
  • Best stacked with GHRP for enhanced GH release
  • Evening injection may improve sleep
  • Rotate injection sites weekly

Common questions

How many units is 2 mg of CJC1295 DAC on an insulin syringe?

40 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 1 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.4 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of CJC1295 DAC?

1 ml is the volume used here, giving 5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is CJC1295 DAC injected?

2x weekly. A typical course runs 12 on, 4 off, which is 24 injections. Reported half-life is 8 days.

How many vials does one CJC1295 DAC cycle take?

About 10 5 mg vials for the full course - 24 doses at 2 mg each, 48 mg in total.

Is CJC1295 DAC approved for human use?

Human trial data. Published human clinical trials exist at comparable doses. The compound is not approved for this use, and trial doses were given under supervision.
