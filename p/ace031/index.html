What ACE 031 is

ACE-031 is a decoy receptor for myostatin and related proteins. Clinical trials showed rapid muscle mass increases (3+ lbs lean mass in 1 month) in muscular dystrophy patients. Acts by binding myostatin before it can inhibit muscle growth.

Mechanism

ACE-031 is a soluble form of the Activin receptor type IIB (ActRIIB) that acts as a decoy receptor. It binds circulating myostatin, activins, and other TGF-beta superfamily members that limit muscle growth, preventing them from signaling to muscle cells.

Common questions

How many units is 1,000 mcg of ACE 031 on an insulin syringe?

40 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 2 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.4 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 5 mg vial of ACE 031?

2 ml is the volume used here, giving 2.5 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 5 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is ACE 031 injected?

Weekly. A typical course runs 4-8 weeks, which is 4-8 injections depending where in that range you dose. Reported half-life is ~15 days.

How many vials does one ACE 031 cycle take?

1-2 5 mg vials for the full course - 4-8 doses at 1,000 mcg each.

Is ACE 031 approved for human use?

Human trial data. Published human clinical trials exist at comparable doses. The compound is not approved for this use, and trial doses were given under supervision.
