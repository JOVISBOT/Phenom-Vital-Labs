Common questions

How many units is 1 mg of BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend (20mg vial) on an insulin syringe?

15 units on a U-100 insulin syringe, when a 20 mg vial is reconstituted with 3 ml of bacteriostatic water. That is 0.15 ml. A U-100 syringe reads 100 units per millilitre whether the barrel holds 30, 50 or 100 units - the barrel size changes how much fits, not what a unit means.

How much bacteriostatic water should I add to a 20 mg vial of BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend (20mg vial)?

3 ml is the volume used here, giving 6.67 mg per ml. Water changes the concentration, not the amount of peptide: the vial holds 20 mg however much you add. More water means a larger, easier-to-read draw for the same dose.

How often is BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend (20mg vial) injected?

5x weekly. A typical course runs 6 on, 4 off, which is 30 injections. Reported half-life is Mixed (4hrs + days).

How many vials does one BPC 157 + TB 500 Blend (20mg vial) cycle take?

About 2 20 mg vials for the full course - 30 doses at 1 mg each, 30 mg in total.
